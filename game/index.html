THE RULEBOOK

🎰
THE CORE LOOP

Build. Spin. Defend.

Minions travel the tracks between your cabinets. Towers fire automatically; your skill is timing spins, placement, upgrades, and Double Down.

1

Read the tracks

Watch both gutters. A tower attacks nearby enemy minions; a minion that crosses the table earns chips for its owner.

2

Work the cabinets

Swipe a cabinet or press SPIN to rotate in a fresh tower from your nine-tower reel strip. Spinning costs 50 match chips.

3

Fill empty windows

Double-tap an empty slot, then place a tower for 80 match chips. Tap a live tower to inspect or upgrade it.

4

Spend with intent

Double Down boosts chip scoring for five seconds. Persistent tower levels and talent trees shape later matches.

How a match ends

The timer reaches zero or one side loses every living tower. Remaining towers and chips decide the winner.
